Why we’re building it

Most good ideas die waiting in a backlog.

Every team has a list of things that would make life better: the internal tool nobody has time for, the small fix that keeps slipping, the feature a customer asked for last spring.

The work isn’t mysterious. It’s a spec, a design, some careful code and a proper review. It just never gets to the top of the pile.

Deplx is our attempt to change that without cutting corners. It does the steps a good team does, in the same order, and it shows its work so you can trust what comes out.

How it works

Same steps a good team takes. You sign off on the big ones.

  1. Tell it what you wantDescribe the idea in a chat, as you would to a colleague.
  2. Approve the planYour callDeplx writes a short spec you can read, change and approve. Nothing is built until you say yes.
  3. Approve the designYour callIt proposes how the software should be built, with security thought through up front. You approve that too.
  4. Tests first, then the buildThe checks are written before the code, so “done” means something. The work happens in its own copy of your project, never on top of your files.
  5. A second pair of eyesSeparate reviewers look at the result with fresh eyes, for design, security and quality. If they go round three times without agreeing, Deplx stops and asks you.
  6. You decide what shipsYour callNothing goes out without your OK, and anything that can’t be undone always waits for you.

Staying in control

Built for people who don’t like surprises.

You can pause any piece of work, send it back a step, or stop it. Every decision is written down, including the ones it made on your behalf, so you can always see why something happened.

It only touches the project you point it at. It hides secrets like passwords and keys from its records. And it works within spending limits you set, so a busy day never turns into a surprise bill.

Working today

  • The full path from idea to reviewed, tested code, with your approvals along the way
  • Several projects side by side, each piece of work in its own copy
  • Pause, resume, send back, and a full history of every decision
  • Spending limits per step, per task and per day
  • Runs on your own computer, on your own projects

What we’re working toward

  • Starting from the tools your team already uses for tickets and docs
  • Learning an existing codebase, not just new projects
  • Shipping straight into your own cloud account
  • Updates where your team already talks
