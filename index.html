About

Hi, I’m Promod.

I’ve spent more than 25 years keeping large systems running at Yahoo, InMobi, Walmart and Affirm, and I built site reliability teams from scratch along the way.

The thing I remember most isn’t the outages. It’s the talented people who lost evenings to work that shouldn’t have needed them: chasing a bug that a closer review would have caught, wiring up the same plumbing for the fifth time, waiting weeks for something that should have taken days.

Techyodl is my answer to that. We build practical tools, we use them ourselves first, and we only claim what we can show. If any of this sounds useful to you, I’d genuinely love to hear from you.
