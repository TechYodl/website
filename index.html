About

Hi, I’m Promod.

I’ve spent more than 25 years keeping large systems running at Yahoo, InMobi, Walmart and Affirm. Along the way, I built site reliability teams from scratch.

Over those years I watched talented engineers lose hours to work a machine could do: chasing bugs late in review, wiring up the same plumbing again and again, waiting on infrastructure.

Techyodl is my answer to that. We build practical tools, we use them ourselves first, and we only claim what we can show. If any of this sounds useful to you, I’d genuinely like to hear from you.
